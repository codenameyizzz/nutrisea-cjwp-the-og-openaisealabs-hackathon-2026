PERSONA-BASED ONBOARDING QUESTIONS
Food Recommendation Feature

Purpose:
These questions help determine a user's primary persona type during onboarding. Use answers as signals, not as a rigid diagnosis or scoring test.

Q1. What is your main goal when choosing food?
Type: Single choice
- Keep my energy steady and choose a balanced meal.
- Eat healthier and reduce my risk of future health problems.
- Build muscle or support my fitness progress.
- Choose food that is gentle on my stomach.
- I'm not sure yet.

Q2. Which best describes your current food-related concern?
Type: Single choice
- I often feel sleepy, sluggish, or too full after lunch.
- I want to be more careful about things like salt, sugar, or fat.
- I want enough protein without spending too much.
- Some foods can trigger stomach discomfort, acidity, or heartburn.
- None of these.

Q3. Do you have any dietary needs or restrictions we should consider?
Type: Single choice; show follow-up if applicable
- I need to limit salt.
- I need to be mindful of sugar or blood glucose.
- I need to be mindful of cholesterol or saturated fat.
- I need food that is gentle on my stomach.
- I have a food allergy or intolerance.
- I don't have any specific dietary restrictions.
- Prefer not to say.

Conditional follow-up:
Is this based on guidance from a healthcare professional, or is it a personal preference?
- Guidance from a healthcare professional
- Personal preference
- Prefer not to say

Note: Use this to guide food filtering, not to diagnose a condition. Keep allergy information separate from general dietary preferences.

- I mainly want to choose based on taste.

PERSONA ASSIGNMENT GUIDE

The Overloaded Corporate Professional (CORP_OVERLOADED)
Strong signals:
- Wants a quick, balanced lunch.
- Experiences decision fatigue or post-lunch sluggishness.
- Focuses on maintaining energy and routine.

The Preventive Health Starter (PREVENTIVE_HEALTH)
Strong signals:
- Wants to eat healthier for long-term wellbeing.
- Mentions being mindful of salt, sugar/glucose, cholesterol, or saturated fat.

The Budget-Conscious Fitness Enthusiast (BUDGET_FITNESS)
Strong signals:
- Wants muscle or fitness progress.
- Prioritizes protein and has a limited meal budget.
- Is active or exercises regularly.

The Digestive & Gentle Palate Fighter (DIGESTIVE_GERD)
Strong signals:
- Prioritizes stomach-friendly meals.
- Is concerned about discomfort, acidity, or heartburn.
- Wants gentle foods and preparation styles.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
- Use Q1 and Q2 as the primary classification questions.
- Use Q3 to set dietary safeguards and refine classification.
- Use Q5–Q8 to personalize recommendations; do not let them independently determine persona.
- If answers match multiple archetypes, store a primary and secondary persona (for example, CORP_OVERLOADED + PREVENTIVE_HEALTH).
- If no pattern is strong, assign a neutral/default profile and refine it through later interactions.
- These questions are a proposed onboarding instrument based on the persona dataset; they are not questions already included in the CSV.
